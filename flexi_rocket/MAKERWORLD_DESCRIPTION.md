# 🚀 Flexi Rocket – Print-in-Place Wiggle Rocket (1 Colour, No Supports, No Hardware)

A fully articulated **flexi rocket** that prints flat in one go and wiggles, curls and swooshes like a rocket on a mission. Six segments – flame, tail with fins, body, nose with porthole – joined by **5 print-in-place pin hinges**. No glue, no screws, no magnets, no LEDs, no AMS.

Looks stunning in **milky / pastel / silk single-colour filament** (mine is milky pink) – the smooth flat sides catch the light beautifully.

## Features
- ✅ **Print-in-place** – 0.3 mm hinge clearance, moves straight off the bed
- ✅ **Zero supports** – prints flat, all walls vertical, only 0.4 mm bridge gaps
- ✅ **One colour, one filament** – no colour changes at all
- ✅ **No extra hardware**
- ✅ **Bambu Lab A1 mini friendly** – 170 × 52 × 8 mm, prints in a couple of hours
- ✅ Fully parametric Python source (numpy + trimesh) included for remixing

## Print settings
- 0.4 mm nozzle, **0.2 mm layers**, 3 walls, 15 % infill, Supports **OFF**
- Elephant-foot compensation **0.1–0.15 mm** (keeps the first-layer gaps open)
- Print slowly (outer walls ~60 mm/s), PLA, no brim needed
- If a hinge feels tight, raise `GAP` to 0.35 in the script and re-export

## After printing
1. Flex each hinge a few times to break the first-layer bond.
2. Wiggle, curl and fly it around your desk. 🚀
