# 🚀 Glow Rocket Lantern – Twist to Launch the Light (Print-in-Place, No Supports, ONE Colour)

A rocket night light that **glows**. Twist the print-in-place shutter ring and six portholes open, flooding your room with soft light. Designed for **single-colour printing** – it looks best in **milky / translucent / silk filament** (mine is milky pink!) where thin walls glow like frosted glass.

## Features
- 🔄 **Print-in-place rotating shutter** – 0.3 mm radial clearance, zero assembly
- 💡 **Fits a 38 mm LED tealight** – the rocket simply sits over the light
- 🚫 **No supports** – teardrop portholes, 45° nose shoulder, 45° engine bell
- 🎨 **No AMS, no colour swaps** – one filament, one print, ~128 mm tall
- 🔧 Fully parametric OpenSCAD file included (hole count, size, height, clearance)

## Print settings (Bambu Lab A1 mini)
- 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layer, **3 walls**, 5 % infill (it's mostly shell), Supports **OFF**
- Brim: outer only; elephant-foot compensation 0.1–0.15 mm
- If the ring sticks, set `gap` to 0.35 in the .scad and re-export
- Use **LED** tealights only (no flame)

## After printing
1. Twist the sleeve to break the first-layer bond.
2. Place the rocket over an LED tealight.
3. Twist the shutter: closed = soft glow, open = full launch light. 🚀
