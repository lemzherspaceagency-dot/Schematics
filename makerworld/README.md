# MakerWorld starter pack (Bambu A1 mini)

Original parametric designs written in OpenSCAD. Edit the variables at the top of each `.scad` file, then export a new STL
(`openscad -o out.stl file.scad`) or open the `.scad` in OpenSCAD. All parts fit the A1 mini's 180x180x180 mm bed and print without supports.

| File | Size (mm) | Orientation | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `headphone_hook` | 58 x 73 x 30 | Lay on its side (profile flat on the bed) | Screw-mount under a desk. Two M4 / #8 countersunk screws. 4 walls, 25% gyroid infill, PETG or PLA+ |
| `cable_clips` | 60 x 52 x 16 | As modeled, flat | 3/5/7 mm clips plus a 3-slot desk organizer. Add double-sided tape under the pads |
| `phone_stand` | 131 x 80 x 75 | As modeled, flat | Fits phones and small tablets, has a cable slot. 3 walls, 15% infill |

`preview_profiles.png` shows the side profile of the hook and stand.

## Before you post
1. Print each one and check the fit (a clip that doesn't hold a 5 mm cable is a bad listing). Adjust `gap_ratio`, `wall`, `angle` and similar variables if needed.
2. Take real photos of the printed parts in use. Listings with good photos get far more downloads.
3. Use a title that matches what people search for, for example "Under-desk headphone hook, screw mount, no supports".
4. In the description, say the print settings, the orientation and that no supports are needed.
5. Pick a license such as CC BY-NC if you want credit and no commercial reuse.

## MakerWorld points and money
MakerWorld gives points and rewards based on downloads, prints and boosts. What you can redeem points for (store credit, filament, gift cards, cash payouts) depends on your region and changes over time, so check MakerWorld's current rewards page before counting on cash.
